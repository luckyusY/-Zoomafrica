Extreme heat, flooding, and water stress are reshaping city planning. The strongest strategies treat resilience as a **systems problem**: infrastructure, public health, and land use moving together.

## Key moves

- Heat mapping + targeted shade corridors  
- Stormwater capture in public space design  
- Early-warning and community response networks
